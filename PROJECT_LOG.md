# ✅ v0.8 — Cake Scene

## Completed

Added the birthday cake celebration scene after the balloon sequence.

### Cake Entrance

The cake smoothly appears in the center using:

- Fade-in
- Scale animation
- Upward movement
- Soft glowing effect

### Candles

Three candles are placed on the cake.

They light up one by one with:

- Flame glow
- Flickering animation
- Different timing between candles

### Cake Interaction

Message:

> Let's cut the cake, Madam Ji ❤️

Button:

> 🔪 Cut Cake

Clicking the button triggers the cake-cutting animation.

### Cake Celebration

The original emoji particle explosion was replaced with a more elegant
falling-star effect.

After cutting the cake, golden star fragments fall from the top of the screen.

Star particles have randomized:

- Horizontal position
- Starting position
- Size
- Falling speed
- Animation delay
- Horizontal drift
- Rotation

The stars use:

- ✦
- ✧
- ⋆

This creates a softer magical effect instead of having all particles
move outward from the cake as a group.

### Completion Message

Then:

A button appears:

> 🎁 One More Surprise

The button is currently prepared for the next scene.

9. Scene 9: Radiant Final Celebration Screen with replay button
